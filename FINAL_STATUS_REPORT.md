## 🎮 How to Play

### Main Game
1. Launch game → Home screen appears
2. Tap to continue → Main menu
3. Select game mode (Classic, Timed, Multiplayer, Zen, Survival, Competitive)
4. Select difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Insane)
5. Click "PLAY NOW"
6. Use arrow keys/WASD or touch D-Pad to control snake
7. Eat food (red dots) to grow and score points
8. Avoid walls, yourself, and obstacles
9. Collect power-ups for special effects
10. Try to beat your high score!

### Competitive Mode
1. Select "Competitive" mode
2. Choose "Ranked" or "Unranked"
3. Play against bot opponent
4. Win matches to increase ELO
5. Climb through 7 rank tiers
6. Track your stats and win rate

### Mini-Games
1. Click "Games" from main menu
2. Select any of 6 mini-games
3. Each has unique gameplay mechanics
4. Compete for high scores!
