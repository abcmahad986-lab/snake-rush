# 🎭 Hero Equipping - Quick Summary

## ✅ You Can Now Equip Heroes!

**Your equipped character and skin will appear in the game!**

---

## 🎮 How to Equip

### Step 1: Open Heroes Screen
- Click **"Heroes"** (🎭) button from main menu

### Step 2: Select Character
- Browse all 25 available characters
- Click on any character you own (not locked)
- Characters unlock as you level up (Level 1-25)

### Step 3: Equip Character
- Click **"Equip"** button
- Character is now your active hero
- Your snake will use this character's colors in-game!

### Step 4: Choose Skin (Optional)
- Each character has 3 different skins
- Select a skin from the list
- Click **"Equip Skin"**
- Your snake colors change immediately!

---

## 🎨 What Changes In-Game

### Before Equipping
- Snake uses default Classic Snake colors (green)

### After Equipping
- Snake uses your equipped character's skin colors
- Head has unique glow effect
- Body has gradient colors
- Fully customized appearance!

### Example
**Equip Fire Dragon + Inferno Skin:**
- Head: Bright orange with red glow
- Body: Orange to red gradient
- Tail: Fades to dark red
- Appearance: Fiery dragon snake!

## 🎮 Skin Unlock Methods

### 1. Level Skin
- Automatic when you reach character's level
- Example: Fire Dragon's "Inferno" skin at level 5

### 2. Chest Skin
- Random drop from chests
- Open Wooden, Silver, Golden, or Legendary chests

### 3. Achievement Skin
- Complete specific achievements
- Earn special skins as rewards
